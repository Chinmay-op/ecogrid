## The Problem: You Are Running Your Energy Operations Blind

Energy makes up **15–30%+ of your operating cost** — yet every critical decision is made *after* the monthly bill arrives.

Three structural problems compound inside that blind spot:

**The Penalty Trap**
Indian HT industrial tariffs average peak demand over a rolling 15-minute window. A single brief spike locks in penal rates of **1.5–2×** for the entire month. Manual reporting cannot prevent this.

**The Silent Waste**
Oversized and idling induction motors consume roughly **66% of your total power**. Compressed-air leaks silently waste **20–30% of compressor output**. None of it shows up on a dashboard — so none of it gets fixed.

**The Unclaimed Prize**
Genuine green efforts go unmonetised. Missing new CCTS carbon rules triggers a **2× non-recoverable penalty**.

> *The demand-charge line is the single most controllable risk on an industrial energy bill. It is driven by scheduling and power factor — not by unavoidable production. That means prediction and automation can eliminate it.*

## The Platform: Physical Reality In. Financial Value Out.

OmniView IQ is a single operating system — not a toolbox — that carries a facility from a raw kilowatt to a tradeable carbon asset. Five integrated layers, one pane of glass.

### Layer 1 — SENSE
Smart meters, thermal sensors, and vibration monitors capturing reality at the source.
- Smart energy meters (Class 0.5S / 0.2S, IEC 62053-22)
- RTD / thermocouple inputs for thermal & combustion
- MEMS triaxial accelerometers for rotating equipment
- Ultrasonic flow meters & leak locators for compressed air
- Flue-gas analyzers (NDIR + electrochemical) for emissions

### Layer 2 — CONNECT
Secure edge gateways normalizing protocols (Modbus, OPC-UA, BACnet) without ripping and replacing hardware.
- Protocol translation to standard MQTT-JSON / HTTPS
- Sub-second local load curtailment without cloud dependency
- Store-and-forward buffering through network outages
- TLS/SSL encryption + built-in VPN, full OT/IT isolation
- Rugged DIN-rail, −40 to 85°C, 4G/5G/LoRaWAN ready

### Layer 3 — MODEL
A live, FMI-compliant **Digital Twin** mirroring the facility's energy profile. Test setpoint changes, batch reschedules, or new motors and see the kWh, rupee, and CO₂ impact *before* touching the plant.

### Layer 4 — INTELLIGENCE
AI forecasting, ROI-ranked recommendations, and automated demand response — continuously learning.
- LSTM + XGBoost demand forecasting on 24-hour horizon
- Optimisation engine with ROI-prioritised recommendations
- Anomaly detection for silent waste and equipment drift
- Automated demand response via edge — sub-second curtailment

### Layer 5 — MONETISE
Automated dMRV, ISO 14064 accounting, blockchain audit trails, and tradeable carbon credits.
- Automated CO₂e accounting using ISO 14064 methods
- Hyperledger Fabric blockchain audit trail (tamper-evident)
- CCTS / ICM registry-API reporting, verifier-ready
- dMRV cuts verification cost **50–70%** and compresses timelines from years to days
